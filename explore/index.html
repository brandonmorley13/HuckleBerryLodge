Duck Creek Village is the basecamp

National parks, ski slopes, and forest trails all sit within a morning's drive.

By Season

There's a reason to come back every quarter

Winter

Winter

Skiing and snowboarding at Brian Head, plus groomed snowmobile trails from the driveway.

Spring

Spring

Shoulder-season quiet, melting snowpack waterfalls, and lower Zion crowds.

Summer

Summer

Lift-served mountain biking, ATV trails, and cool mountain evenings while the valley bakes.

Fall

Fall

Aspen color through Cedar Breaks and Dixie National Forest, usually peaking late September.

Area Guide

Where to go from here

Brian Head Resort

Brian Head Resort

Utah's highest-base ski resort, 20 minutes from the cabin.

Cedar Breaks National Monument

Cedar Breaks National Monument

A 2,000-foot amphitheater of red rock, minutes from the driveway.

Duck Creek Trail System

Duck Creek Trail System

Snowmobile and ATV trails accessible straight from the village.

Zion National Park

Zion National Park

The east entrance is about 90 minutes down Highway 14 and 89.

Bryce Canyon National Park

Bryce Canyon National Park

Hoodoos and sunrise-point overlooks, under two hours out.

Cedar City

Cedar City

Groceries, restaurants, an airport, and the Utah Shakespeare Festival.

Navajo Lake

Navajo Lake

Fishing, kayaking, and a summer boat ramp fifteen minutes away.

Panguitch Lake

Panguitch Lake

A quieter fishing lake with cabins and a general store.
