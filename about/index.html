Our Story

We built this cabin for our own reunions first

Huckle Berry Lodge started as a family project: a place big enough that nobody had to stay home. After a few years of hosting our own reunions here, we opened it up to other families looking for the same thing — room enough that everyone actually gets to be together.

We live nearby and manage every stay ourselves, from the first message to the final walkthrough.
