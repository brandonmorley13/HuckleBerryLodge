Policies

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in is 4:00 PM, check-out is 10:00 AM. Early arrival and late departure are available on request, subject to the calendar.

Occupancy

Maximum 24 guests. Events, weddings, and parties require advance approval — message us before booking.

Payment

Full payment is collected through Airbnb or VRBO at the time of booking; no separate payment is required.

Damage Deposit

A refundable deposit is authorized at booking and released after a post-stay walkthrough finds no damage.
