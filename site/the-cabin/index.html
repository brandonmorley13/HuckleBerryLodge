The Cabin

Three floors built for a full house

A floor-by-floor look at where everyone sleeps, gathers, and unwinds.

Huckle Berry Lodge sleeps up to 24 across ten bedrooms, with living space stacked over three levels so groups can spread out by day and come back together for dinner. Below is the layout floor by floor.

Main level great room
Main Level

The great room, kitchen & dining table for 16

Vaulted log ceilings, a wood-burning stove, and a kitchen island built for a crowd. The dining table seats sixteen, with overflow bar seating at the island.

Upper Level

Five bedrooms and a reading loft

Quiet, carpeted, and set back from the great room noise — the upper level holds five bedrooms plus a small loft with a window seat overlooking the ridge.

Upper level loft
Lower level game room
Lower Level

Game room, theater & a walkout patio

This is where the group ends up after dinner: a pool table, arcade games, a theater room, and a second kitchenette for late-night snacks, all opening onto a walkout patio and the hot tub.
